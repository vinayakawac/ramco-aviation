02 / The Problem

Your aircraft got smarter.
Your systems didn't.

Maintenance is in one system. Engineering is in another. Supply chain, contracts and finance are each in a third. Every handover between them is where a day gets lost, a part gets ordered twice, or a job gets done and never invoiced.

01

Estimates that miss

Fixed-price work quoted before the panels come off. Non-routine findings arrive after the number is agreed.

02

No resource visibility

Manual work scheduling against a workforce and a parts position nobody can see in real time.

03

Inventory leakage

Capital parked in bins nobody can account for, while the same part gets bought again.

04

Revenue that leaks

Scrap limits exceeded without approval. Upgrade differentials never charged. Caps breached unnoticed.
